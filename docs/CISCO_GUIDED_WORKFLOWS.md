# Cisco Guided Workflows

Milestone 18.2A does not add executable Cisco mutations.

Future guided workflows must use:

- Detected platform and supported version.
- Inventory-derived interface/VLAN/Port-Channel options.
- Strict schemas and allowlists.
- Preflight, preview, confirmation, backup, connector execution, verification and rollback status.
- ActionPlan handoff and Action Center confirmation.

The EtherChannel and VLAN mutations remain planned until lab verification and rollback strategy exist.
