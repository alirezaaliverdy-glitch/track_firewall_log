# Simple NVR registration and inventory deletion

Choose the company, enter the NVR's reachable private IPv4 address and device account, then select Add and check connection. NVR registration defaults to Automatic; a protocol choice is not required. The optional advanced section exposes custom web port/HTTPS and explicit ONVIF, AppWeb, DVRIP or RTSP configuration.

Automatic detection makes only two fixed, bounded unauthenticated GET probes: AppWeb's known common script and the configured ONVIF service path. AppWeb identification requires both session and CGI markers. No password spraying, port sweep, redirect following or device-configuration change occurs. Once identified, only the selected existing connector authenticates. Incorrect credentials do not trigger attempts against other protocols. Custom HTTPS keeps normal certificate validation.

An unknown/offline brand can still be saved as inventory. It remains explicitly unverified/error with `NVR_AUTO_DETECT_UNAVAILABLE`; no fake channels, disk health or successful monitoring are reported. Inventory registration across brands does not mean universal sensor/configuration/playback support. Proprietary/nonstandard devices may need their advanced port/protocol or a future vendor connector. A reachable server route (direct network or the selected VPN) is still necessary. This is not Cloud ID/P2P support.

Deleting a recorder through cameras or its projected NVR asset removes its CameraDevice transport and archives its inventory projection. The projection's management IP is released and preserved as historical `formerManagementIp`, allowing re-registration without erasing historical inventory. Registration also repairs pre-fix archived orphan NVR reservations: only a positively identified NVR projection whose CameraDevice no longer exists is eligible. Live NVRs and unrelated/generic assets retain collision protection. Tenant, company, CSRF and role checks remain in force; viewers cannot register, delete or change equipment. No hardware recordings are deleted.

Tests cover the automatic SOAP fixture, unknown-device persistence without credential transmission, camera-only operator grants, viewer denial, both deletion entry points, legacy orphan recovery, repeated re-registration and live/generic collisions. UI fixtures verify dark/light desktop and 390/320px registration with advanced choices optional. No schema migration or production data cleanup is required for this repair.
